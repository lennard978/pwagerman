# German B1 Speaking source record

Reviewed: 2026-10-31

## Structural references

- Goethe-Institut, Goethe-Zertifikat B1 exam information: https://www.goethe.de/ins/de/de/prf/gzb1.cfm
- Goethe-Institut, accessible B1 Speaking model: https://bfu.goethe.de/b1_mod/sprechen.php
- Goethe-Institut, current B1 examination regulations (`Durchführungsbestimmungen`): https://www.goethe.de/pro/relaunch/prf/de/Durchfuehrungsbestimmungen_B1.pdf
- Goethe-Institut, current B1 practice materials: https://www.goethe.de/ins/de/de/m/prf/gzb1/ueb.html

The sources were used to verify the three-part structure, 15-minute preparation period, approximate pair and individual durations, task functions, and high-level evaluation dimensions. The application presents all timings as preparation or guidance, not as a guarantee that local practice reproduces a live examination.

## Original-content declaration

- No official speaking scenarios were copied.
- No official presentation topics were copied.
- No official examiner or follow-up questions were copied.
- No official sample performance or scoring sheet was reproduced.
- All scenarios, deterministic partner turns, topic cards, questions, instructions, examples, and guidance in the app are original app-authored content.
- The 0–3 rubric is a learner self-assessment and is not official Goethe scoring. It does not calculate an official score or pass/fail result.
