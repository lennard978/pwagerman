## Provenance rules

- `verified` means a source directly supports the specific scope or item.
- `supplementary` means original app-authored content aligned to the sourced A1 functional scope.
- Current vocabulary pairs remain `supplementary` because no authoritative pair-level A1 list was confirmed.
- Grammar explanations, examples, mistakes, and exercises are app-authored and intentionally concise.
- No course-book passages or official exam questions are reproduced.

## Exam-preparation boundary

The planned Exam Prep areas are Vocabulary, Grammar, Reading, Listening, and Mock A1. Future tasks must use original practice modeled on CEFR A1 skills and must not present app-authored questions as University of Belgrade or official CEFR exam items.
