# German B1 Writing sources and originality record

Reviewed: 2026-10-31

## Official structural references

- Goethe-Zertifikat B1 exam information: https://www.goethe.de/ins/de/de/prf/gzb1.cfm
- Accessible Writing model: https://bfu.goethe.de/b1_mod/schreiben.php
- Current B1 practice materials: https://www.goethe.de/ins/de/de/m/prf/gzb1/ueb.html
- Current Goethe-Zertifikat B1 examination regulations: https://www.goethe.de/pro/relaunch/prf/de/Durchfuehrungsbestimmungen_B1.pdf

These references were used only for the module structure: 60 minutes; three tasks; personal email, discussion contribution, and short formal email; approximate 80/80/40-word guidance; and the broad evaluation dimensions task fulfilment, coherence, vocabulary, and structures.

## Originality and grading boundary

- No official prompt wording was copied.
- No official sample response was copied.
- No examiner text or grading guidance was copied.
- All scenarios, prompts, bullet points, checklists, phrase guidance, and review guidance are original app-authored material.
- The app does not use Goethe branding or claim affiliation or endorsement.
- The 0–3 rubric is a learner self-assessment aid. It is not an official score, pass/fail decision, or certification result.
- Deterministic checks are limited to word/paragraph counts, non-empty text, greeting/closing presence, approximate length, and manually completed checklist state. They do not grade grammar.

## Word-count rule

The app counts Unicode letter or number runs as words. Internal hyphens and apostrophes remain part of one word, so German compounds such as `E-Mail` and forms such as `geht's` count once. Punctuation, repeated spaces, and paragraph breaks do not add words. Approximate targets are guidance rather than hard cutoffs.
